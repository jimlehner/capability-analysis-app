### Global Standard Deviation

The global standard deviation (s) is a measure of the distribution of all the values in a dataset relative to the mean. It is calculated using the formula:

[FIGURE_SD]

Here, X<sub>i</sub> is the i-th value in the dataset, Mean is the average, n is the number of values in the dataset, and Σ(X<sub>i</sub> - Mean)<sup>2</sup> is the sum of the squared deviations of each value in the dataset from the mean.

Unlike the within-subgroup measure of dispersion, Sigma(X), the standard deviation assumes a dataset to be homogeneous by way of its calculation. Thius is why it is used in the calculation of the performance ratios. These ratios relate what a process has done in the past (using the standard deviation) to the tolerance and DNS.
