### Sigma(X)

Sigma(X) is a generic placeholder for one of several within-subgroup measures of dispersion. When a dataset is composed of logically comparable individual values, sigma(X) relates the average moving range to the bias correction factor, d2. Assuming a dataset is composed of logically comparable individual values, the value of d2 = 1.128.
