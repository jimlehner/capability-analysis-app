## Taguchi Loss Calculator (Web App)

The Taguchi loss calculator is a web application that allows users to calculate the economic loss due to poor quality. This model of loss, goverened by the Taguchi loss function, serves as a practical alternative to the conformance to sepcification (spec) model of loss that has dominated industry since the late 19th century.

Under the Taguchi loss function, product of the highest quality is produced at the vertex of the parabola (target) with incremental losses incurred as quality or performance characteristics deviate. These loses increase quadratically until they reach the specification limits at which point the maximum loss due to poor quality is incurred.
