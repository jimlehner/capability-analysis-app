## Centered Capability Ratio, Cpk

The centered capability ratio (Cpk) divides the [effective space available] to a process by the [space required] by the process. The effective space available accounts for how well the space between the specification limits (the tolerance) is utilized by incorporating the mean into its calculation. While the space required is denoted by the quantity 3 · Sigma(X), the effective space available is denoted by the distance to the nearest specification (DNS). The DNS describes the location of the process mean relative to the specification limits.

## Performance Ratio, Pp

The performance ratio (Pp) compares the [space available] to the [space used in the past]. It does this by dividing the tolerance by the global standard deviation statistic (s).
